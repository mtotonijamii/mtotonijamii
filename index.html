A LIFE WE'VE INSPIRED

When a young person discovers their voice.

Joyashley's journey is a reminder of what can happen when a young person is given encouragement, guidance, opportunity and a safe space to discover who they can become.

Through the support and opportunities provided by Mtoto Ni Jamii, Joyashley was encouraged to explore her potential, grow in confidence and embrace the talents and possibilities within her.

Her story represents what we believe at Mtoto Ni Jamii: every young person carries potential, and with the right support, that potential can become a powerful force for their future and their community.
