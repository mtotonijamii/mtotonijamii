WHO WE ARE

Every young person deserves a chance to become.

Mtoto Ni Jamii Foundation works to promote the wellbeing, growth and potential of young people.

Through mentorship, talent development, practical opportunities, climate action and community outreach, we help young people feel valued, discover their strengths and move forward with confidence.
